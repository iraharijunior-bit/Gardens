About Gardens

International gardening solutions provider dedicated to sustainable growth

Growing Together, Naturally

Gardens is an international gardening solutions provider dedicated to helping individuals, families, and organizations grow sustainable and thriving gardens. With carefully curated subscription boxes, educational activities, and expert seasonal guidance, we empower gardeners of all experience levels to cultivate confidence, creativity, and environmental responsibility.

Operating across Europe, Asia, and New Zealand, we bring together diverse gardening traditions with modern sustainable practices, creating a global community of passionate gardeners.

Sustainability Commitment

Our Green Promise

We prioritize sustainability across every part of our operations, from supporting reduced food waste through our Oddbox concept to using eco-friendly packaging and encouraging biodiversity through wildflower and seed initiatives.

Our goal is to help communities grow responsibly while protecting the environment for future generations. Every box we deliver, every seed we plant, and every gardener we support contributes to a more sustainable world.
